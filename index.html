Lights

Left, Right and Hazard hold a signal by hand. Automatic shows an arrow only while the vehicle is really cornering: pushed sideways and turning. Tilting your head does nothing.

Rides

Connect to see the rides stored on the helmet.

A ride starts when you pick the helmet up and ends after it has lain still for two minutes. Sync copies finished rides to your GitHub repository.

Cloud setup

Create the token on github.com → Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens, limited to this one repository, with Contents: Read and write.
